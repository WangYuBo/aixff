关于影展About the Festival

2026AI国际影展是2026“安徽文化中国行”的开篇之作，也是2026“岐黄文学月”第五档系列活动。同期举办的还有2026国际文旅博览会（11月13–15日，主题“文旅无界，商机无限”）。

这是一个把AI技术、人类创意与电影艺术放在同一束光下的舞台 —— 从院线长片到AI微短剧，从银龄创作者到13–17岁的高中生，人人皆可“以AI造梦”。

2026 AI International Film Festival is the opening event of 2026 "Anhui Culture · China Journey" and the fifth program of the 2026 "Qihuang Literature Month". It runs alongside the 2026 International Culture & Tourism Expo (Nov 13–15, "Culture & Tourism Without Borders, Boundless Opportunities").

One stage where AI technology, human creativity and cinematic art share the same light — from feature films to AI micro-dramas, from senior creators to high-school filmmakers aged 13–17, everyone can dream with AI.
