Culture & Technology Symbiosis · Cinema Creates the Future

核心愿景：文技共生 · 影创未来。会议由IEEE中国联合会、AIVTA2026组委会、欧美影视协会、马来西亚测绘大学主办；北美科发出版集团、北京科发影视传媒有限公司承办；水木时代研究院、山东儒之风教育科技有限公司、江西八斗教育科技有限公司、马来西亚华穆多元出版社协办。 Vision: "Culture & Technology Symbiosis · Cinema Creates the Future." Hosted by the IEEE China Joint Council, AIVTA2026 Organizing Committee, Euro-American Film & TV Association and Malaysia Surveying & Mapping University; organized by North American Kefa Publishing Group and Beijing Kefa Film & TV Media; co-organized by Shumu Times Research Institute, Shandong Ruzhifeng Education Technology, Jiangxi Badou Education Technology and Malaysia Huamu Multi-Publisher.

  1. 生成式AI与影视文学创意内容生产Generative AI & creative content production for film & literature
  2. 智能拍摄与虚拟制片技术Intelligent filming & virtual production
  3. 多模态感知与后期制作自动化Multimodal perception & post-production automation
  4. 计算机图形学与视觉特效（VFX）Computer graphics & visual effects (VFX)
  5. 影视文学大数据与智能分发Big data & intelligent distribution
  6. 沉浸式媒体与交互叙事Immersive media & interactive narrative
  7. 合规、伦理治理与法律规制Compliance, ethical governance & legal regulation
  8. 产业变革与人才培养Industry transformation & talent development
