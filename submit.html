参赛作品要求Submission Requirements

作品体裁Accepted Formats

  • AI院线电影AI Feature Film 90–120 分钟90–120 min
  • AI剧情短片AI Narrative Short 5–30 分钟，需完整叙事结构5–30 min, complete narrative
  • AI实验影像AI Experimental 时长形式不限Open duration & form
  • AI动画/动态影像AI Animation / Motion 2D/3D动画、MV、动态图形2D/3D animation, MV, motion graphics
  • AI微短剧/系列剧AI Micro-drama / Series 单集3–15分钟，提交总长不超30分钟3–15 min per episode, total ≤30 min

硬性要求Mandatory Requirements

  • 作品须为原创，所有素材（含AI训练数据）须有合法授权Original work; all materials (incl. AI training data) legally licensed
  • AI生成或AI辅助制作内容须超过50%AI-generated or AI-assisted content must exceed 50%
  • 必须提交《AI技术应用说明文档》（工具、模型、应用环节及工作流），无此文档投稿无效Mandatory "AI Technical Application Statement" (tools, models, stages, workflow) — submissions without it are invalid
  • 格式 MP4/MOV；分辨率≥1920×1080；帧率≥24fps；文件≤20GBFormat MP4/MOV; resolution ≥1920×1080; frame rate ≥24fps; file ≤20GB
  • 中英双语字幕（无对白作品除外）Bilingual CN/EN subtitles (except dialogue-free works)
  • 中英文简介（100–200字）及创作者/团队简介CN/EN synopsis (100–200 words) and creator/team bio

投稿与报名：请邮件联系组委会 wcwpc@foxmail.com，或致电索取报名回执；参赛作品请随稿附《AI技术应用说明文档》。 Submit & register: email the Committee at wcwpc@foxmail.com or call for the registration form; include the AI Technical Application Statement with your work.
